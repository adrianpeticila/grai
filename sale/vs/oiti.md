## 1. Two Different Jobs: Clone and Publish vs. Measure and Gate

Oiti starts with a clone. You connect your LinkedIn and website, and it builds a voice profile, brand pillars and a content strategy in about 30 seconds. It also researches your ICP across X, Reddit and the wider web, then keeps a Knowledge Base and long-term memory for every draft.

`grai` starts with measurement. You paste 5 to 20 posts you actually wrote. It extracts average sentence length, sentence-length variance, openings, endings and signature words. Every later draft is checked against 8 structural rules plus a burstiness floor at `CV >= 0.32`, and the exact failing span is highlighted. The check runs in one file, in the browser. No account. No server.

The purchase decision is not "which tool writes more". It is "do you want a cloud clone that researches and publishes for you, or a local gate that decides whether the draft still sounds like you". Oiti sells the first. `grai` sells the second.

## 2. Where Oiti Wins vs. Where grai Wins

### Where Oiti Wins

- **30-second clone and research:** reads your LinkedIn and website, then researches your ICP's pain points across X, Reddit and the deep web. `grai` requires you to paste samples manually.
- **Knowledge Base and long-term memory:** keeps videos, PDFs, articles, calls and competitor posts as retrievable sources. Every edit and correction is stored for future drafts.
- **Publishing workflow:** writes, designs infographics, schedules and publishes through LinkedIn's official APIs, then measures impressions, reach, followers and engagement. `grai` stops at the draft.
- **Scale on Pro:** up to 3 AI clones, unlimited writing styles, 3 personal LinkedIn accounts and 10+ company accounts. `grai` never connects to LinkedIn.
- **Price of entry and trial:** Creator is $49/month, Pro is $79/month, and both include a 7-day free trial. `grai` has no free trial beyond the free scorer.

### Where grai Wins

- **Deterministic gate:** 8 regex rules plus a `CV >= 0.32` floor, with exact span highlights and targeted repair. The same input returns the same result. Oiti's clone and memory are generation features, not a published mechanical rhythm check.
- **Local ownership:** the shipped `grai.html` runs with `connect-src 'none'`. Profiles and drafts live in browser `localStorage` and portable `.grai` files, not in a vendor workspace.
- **No LinkedIn OAuth:** `grai` never holds a session token and never schedules or publishes. There is no automation surface to restrict.
- **Client proof ($790/yr Agency):** up to 10 isolated client profiles, A4 SHA-256 Client Voice DNA Dossiers and public ECDSA P-256 verification links at `grai.page/verify/:hash`. Oiti's public pricing page does not list a per-client proof artifact.
- **One annual price for 10 client voices:** $790/yr Agency against a per-account cloud subscription. The Agency price does not grow with every LinkedIn account.

## 5. Privacy and Data Flow Boundary

- **Shipped Pre-Built Kit (`grai.html`):** Ships with a Content Security Policy (`default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; font-src data:; img-src data:; script-src 'unsafe-inline'; connect-src 'none'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'none'`) that allows inline styles and inline scripts (plus `data:` fonts and images) while blocking direct API connections (`connect-src 'none'`) from the page. Out of the box, it runs in Copy-Paste mode.
- **Browser Storage:** Voice profiles, samples and drafts are stored in your browser's `localStorage` when available. If your browser blocks `localStorage` on local `file://` pages, `grai` falls back to in-memory session storage, which lasts until the tab closes. Export a `.grai` backup file to keep the profile permanently on disk.
- **When data leaves your machine:** only when you paste a prompt into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Copy-Paste mode, when you run a custom source build with BYOK and send a prompt to your chosen provider, or when you export and share a `.grai` file or PDF Dossier yourself. Oiti keeps the clone, Knowledge Base, drafts, schedule and analytics inside its cloud workspace by design.
- **Language scope:** voice extraction works in any language you paste. The 8 deterministic structural rules are tuned for English. Test a non-English draft before you rely on the rule count.

## 6. Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

**Choose Oiti ($49/mo Creator or $79/mo Pro) if:** the job is LinkedIn growth and you want a cloud clone that researches your audience, keeps memory, designs infographics, schedules through the official API and reports analytics. The 7-day trial lets you test the workflow before paying.

**Choose grai ($79/yr Solo, or $99/mo or $790/yr Agency) if:** the job is voice fidelity and proof. You want a deterministic 8-rule + `CV >= 0.32` gate, exact span repairs, portable `.grai` profiles, and up to 10 client Dossiers with public ECDSA verification for one flat annual price.

**Use both if:** Oiti handles research, drafts and scheduling, and `grai` runs the final cadence check before anything is queued. The two do not overlap.

**Do not buy grai if:** you want a 7-day free trial, a LinkedIn clone, ICP research, a Knowledge Base, infographics, official API scheduling, analytics, multiple LinkedIn accounts, or deterministic structural checks in a language other than English. Oiti is the better buy for those jobs.

### How does Oiti pricing compare with grai?

Oiti costs $49 per month on Creator and $79 per month on Pro, with a 7-day free trial. grai Solo is $79 per year, and the Agency Verification Protocol is $99 per month or $790 per year for up to 10 isolated client profiles. ### Does Oiti include a deterministic cadence checker?

No. Oiti builds a cloud AI clone from your LinkedIn and website, researches your ICP, keeps a Knowledge Base and long-term memory, then writes, designs, schedules and measures LinkedIn content. grai runs 8 structural regex rules plus a `CV >= 0.32` burstiness floor in the browser, highlights the exact spans that fail, and offers targeted repair.

### Can I use Oiti for scheduling and grai for the voice check?

Yes. Use Oiti to research, draft and schedule on LinkedIn, then paste the final draft into grai or the free scorer at https://grai.page/#test. grai returns the measured cadence and the failing spans. grai never connects to LinkedIn and never publishes anything.
