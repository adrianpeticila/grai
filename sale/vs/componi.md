## 1. Two Different Jobs: Publishing Autopilot vs. Voice Gate

Componi starts with the publishing loop. You build a Voice Profile, generate posts in the Post Engine, plan a week in Content Studio, schedule through Autopilot, answer comments in the Engagement Inbox, and read the result in Analytics. The promise is output: more posts, less manual work, one dashboard.

`grai` starts with measurement. You paste 5 to 20 posts you actually wrote. It extracts a quantitative Voice DNA profile: average sentence length, sentence-length variance, openings, endings, signature words. Every later draft is checked against 8 structural rules plus a burstiness floor at `CV >= 0.32`, and the exact failing span is highlighted in the text. The check runs in one file, in the browser. No account. No server.

The purchase decision is not "which tool writes more". It is "do you want a cloud autopilot that publishes for you, or a local gate that decides whether the draft sounds like you". Componi sells the first. `grai` sells the second.

## 2. Where Componi Wins vs. Where grai Wins

### Where Componi Wins

- **Price of entry:** the 90-day free beta costs nothing and needs no card. Pro Standard is €12/month, Ambassador is €9.99/month for life, and Founding is €4.99/month for life for the first 200 users. `grai` has no free tier.
- **Publishing workflow:** Post Engine, Content Studio, Weekly Plan, 6-level Autopilot and Engagement Inbox cover the work from idea to published post and comments. `grai` stops at the draft.
- **Unlimited LinkedIn accounts:** the Standard plan lists unlimited LinkedIn accounts. `grai` never connects to LinkedIn at all.
- **Native languages:** Componi lists 10 native languages for the product and the content workflow. `grai` extracts a voice profile in any language, but its 8 deterministic structural rules are tuned for English.
- **EU positioning:** GDPR compliance, EU data residency and SOC 2 in progress are stated on the pricing page. Useful for buyers who want a European vendor.

### Where grai Wins

- **Deterministic gate:** 8 regex rules plus a `CV >= 0.32` floor, with exact span highlights and targeted repair. The same input returns the same result. Componi's Voice Profile is a generation feature, not a mechanical rhythm check.
- **Local ownership:** the shipped `grai.html` runs with `connect-src 'none'`. Profiles and drafts live in browser `localStorage` and portable `.grai` files, not in a vendor workspace.
- **No LinkedIn OAuth:** `grai` never holds a session token and never schedules or publishes. There is no automation surface to restrict.
- **Client proof ($790/yr Agency):** up to 10 isolated client profiles, A4 SHA-256 Client Voice DNA Dossiers and public ECDSA P-256 verification links at `grai.page/verify/:hash`. Componi's pricing page does not list a per-client proof artifact.
- **One annual price for 10 client voices:** $790/yr Agency against a per-account cloud subscription. The Agency price does not grow with every client profile.

## 5. Privacy and Data Flow Boundary

- **Shipped Pre-Built Kit (`grai.html`):** Ships with a Content Security Policy (`default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; font-src data:; img-src data:; script-src 'unsafe-inline'; connect-src 'none'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'none'`) that allows inline styles and inline scripts (plus `data:` fonts and images) while blocking direct API connections (`connect-src 'none'`) from the page. Out of the box, it runs in Copy-Paste mode.
- **Browser Storage:** Voice profiles, samples and drafts are stored in your browser's `localStorage` when available. If your browser blocks `localStorage` on local `file://` pages, `grai` falls back to in-memory session storage, which lasts until the tab closes. Export a `.grai` backup file to keep the profile permanently on disk.
- **When data leaves your machine:** only when you paste a prompt into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Copy-Paste mode, when you run a custom source build with BYOK and send a prompt to your chosen provider, or when you export and share a `.grai` file or PDF Dossier yourself. Componi keeps the profile, drafts, schedule and analytics inside its cloud workspace by design.
- **Language scope:** voice extraction works in any language you paste. The 8 deterministic structural rules are tuned for English. Test a non-English draft before you rely on the rule count.

## 6. Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

**Choose Componi (€12/mo, or €4.99/mo Founding while it lasts) if:** the job is publishing volume and you want a cloud workflow from idea to post. The 90-day beta removes the trial risk, and the Autopilot plus Engagement Inbox save real hours if LinkedIn is your main channel.

**Choose grai ($79/yr Solo, or $99/mo or $790/yr Agency) if:** the job is voice fidelity and proof. You want a deterministic 8-rule + `CV >= 0.32` gate, exact span repairs, portable `.grai` profiles, and up to 10 client Dossiers with public ECDSA verification for one flat annual price.

**Use both if:** Componi handles planning and publishing, and `grai` runs the final cadence check before anything is queued. The two do not overlap.

**Do not buy grai if:** you want a free 90-day beta, a €4.99 lifetime price, automatic LinkedIn posting, an engagement inbox, analytics, unlimited LinkedIn accounts, or deterministic structural checks in a language other than English. Componi is the better buy for those jobs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Componi pricing compare with grai?

Componi offers a 90-day free beta with no card, then Pro Standard at €12 per month, Pro Ambassador at €9.99 per month for life with a referral, and Founding Member at €4.99 per month for life for the first 200 users. grai Solo is $79 per year, and the Agency Verification Protocol is $99 per month or $790 per year for up to 10 isolated client profiles. ### Does Componi include a deterministic cadence checker?

No. Componi generates and schedules content inside its cloud workspace with a Voice Profile, Post Engine, Content Studio, Weekly Plan, Autopilot, Engagement Inbox and Analytics. grai runs 8 structural regex rules plus a `CV >= 0.32` burstiness floor in the browser, highlights the exact spans that fail, and offers targeted repair.

### Can I use Componi for scheduling and grai for the voice check?

Yes. Use Componi to plan and publish on LinkedIn, then paste the draft into grai or the free scorer at https://grai.page/#test. grai returns the measured cadence and the failing spans. grai never connects to LinkedIn and never schedules anything.
