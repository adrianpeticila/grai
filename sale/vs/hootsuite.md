## 1. Governance vs. Voice

Hootsuite sells control. Approvals, roles, an audit trail and a scheduler that a large marketing department can run are worth a monthly fee, and the templates in OwlyWriter AI keep a distributed team inside the brand line.

That is also the limit. A template produces the brand line, not the executive. When a founder or a CMO publishes through a corporate template, the post reads like the company and not like a person, and the reach reflects it.

`grai` measures the person. It extracts a profile from 5 to 20 real posts, gates each draft against that profile, and prints an A4 dossier for the client or the communications team. It does not schedule, and it does not try to govern a department.

## 2. Where Hootsuite Wins vs. Where grai Wins

### Where Hootsuite Wins

- **Approvals and roles:** a workflow a large team can audit.
- **Multi-account scheduling:** dozens of profiles in one calendar.
- **Analytics and reporting:** numbers a marketing department reports upward.
- **Templates:** OwlyWriter AI keeps output inside a brand line.

### Where grai Wins

- **One person, measured:** the profile comes from the executive's own posts, not a brand template.
- **Deterministic gate:** 8 rules and a `CV >= 0.32` floor, with the failed spans named.
- **A fraction of the price:** $79/yr or $99/mo or $790/yr against $1,188 to $2,988 per year.
- **Local by default:** the text stays on the machine, which matters for executive drafts.

## 5. Privacy and Data Flow Boundary

- **Shipped Pre-Built Kit (`grai.html`):** Ships with a Content Security Policy (`default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; font-src data:; img-src data:; script-src 'unsafe-inline'; connect-src 'none'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'none'`) that allows inline styles and inline scripts (plus `data:` fonts and images) while blocking direct API connections (`connect-src 'none'`) from the page. Out of the box, it runs in Copy-Paste mode.
- **Browser Storage:** Voice profiles and writing samples (along with drafts) are stored in your browser's `localStorage` when available. If your browser settings or private-browsing mode block `localStorage` on local `file://` pages, `grai` falls back to in-memory session storage (`memStorage`), which lasts until the tab closes. Export a `.grai` backup file to save your profiles permanently to disk.
- **When data leaves your machine:** only when you paste a prompt into a model in Copy-Paste mode, when a custom source build calls a BYOK provider, or when you send a dossier to a client. Hootsuite stores drafts, approvals and account connections in its cloud by design.

## 6. Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

**Choose Hootsuite ($99 to $249 per month) if:** you run a department, need approvals and governance, and report social numbers upward every month.

**Choose grai ($79/yr) if:** the executive's voice is the asset and a template is the failure mode. One measured profile and a floor do more for reach than another calendar.

**Use both if:** Hootsuite governs the calendar and grai gates the drafts before they enter it. The two do not overlap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does grai replace Hootsuite?

No. Hootsuite schedules, approves and reports across many accounts. grai measures a voice and audits a draft locally. It never connects to a social account, and it cannot publish anything on your behalf.

### How does the cost compare over two years?

Hootsuite costs $2,376 to $5,976 over 24 months depending on the tier. grai Solo Offline is $79/yr and the Agency Verification Protocol is $99/mo or $790/yr, with annual or monthly billing.

### Can grai work alongside OwlyWriter AI?

Yes. Paste the OwlyWriter draft into the local bench and run the audit. The gate reports whether the template preserved the executive's cadence or flattened it.
